Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PayFlow (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and services located at https://payflow-wqno.onrender.com (the “Service”).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Bank Account Information: When you link a bank account, we collect information necessary to verify and process payments. This may include bank name, account type, last four digits of the account number, and routing number. Full account details are handled securely by our payment processor (Stripe) and are not stored in plain text on our servers.
  • Transaction Information: Details about transfers, deposits, and withdrawals you make through the Service.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information, and usage data collected automatically when you visit the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • Verify bank accounts (including through micro-deposit or instant verification methods)
  • Process payments, transfers, and withdrawals
  • Communicate with you about your account and transactions
  • Improve and personalize the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud

3. Payment Processing

We use Stripe as our payment processor. When you link a bank account or make a transfer, your payment information is processed by Stripe. Stripe’s use of your personal information is governed by their own Privacy Policy, available at https://stripe.com/privacy.

We do not store full bank account numbers or sensitive payment credentials on our own servers. Tokenized or limited information is stored only as needed to provide the Service.

4. How We Share Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Such as Stripe (payment processing), hosting providers, and analytics tools that help us operate the Service.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the email address below.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the “Last updated” date.
